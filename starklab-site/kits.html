The guardrails

What a kit never does.

The same three rules in every kit, written into the install rather than left to good intentions.

01 · Never acts alone

Nothing is sent, posted or changed without a named human approving it.

A kit drafts. It never sends a reply, posts a message, edits a record or changes a setting until the approver on your team says yes. The approval step is part of the install, not an option you can skip.

02 · Never acts unseen

Every action is logged, with who approved it and when.

Each run records what the kit read, what it drafted, who approved it and the time. You can open the log on any day. Alerts go out when a case is uncertain, a score drops or a connection fails.

03 · Never leaves you without a way back

Every approved action can be reversed from the log.

The undo path: each approved action is recorded with exactly what it changed, so your team can reverse it from the log. Where a connected tool has no way to reverse an action, the kit only drafts for that tool and a person completes it.

Questions

Asked before every kit.

Straight answers. If yours is not here, ask it on the call.

Can it learn our tone?

Yes, within limits you can see. On day one we take twenty of your real cases with the replies your best person would send, and the kit is configured against them. The day-two score shows how close the tone is before anything goes live. It does not change on its own after that: adjustments to tone go through us, or through the care plan, and every change is re-scored on the same twenty cases.

What if accuracy is below the bar?

The bar is the score agreed on day one on your twenty cases. If the kit is under it on day two, we tune and re-score before the approval step is switched on, so nothing reaches a customer below the bar. If your cases turn out to need a different build, we say so with the failing cases in front of you and quote the change as a fixed price before you commit further.

What happens after the four days?

The kit is live and yours: the configuration, the log and the accounts are in your name. Most teams add the Agent care plan at the catalogue price for monitoring, a monthly report and fixes. Without it the kit keeps running as installed, and changes are quoted as a fixed price when you ask for them.

Can we cancel the care plan?

Yes. It is billed month by month, and one message stops it. The kit keeps running as it is; what stops is the monitoring, the monthly report and the fixes. You can start it again later without a new install.
